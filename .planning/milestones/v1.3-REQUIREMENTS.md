## v1.3 Requirements

### Zoom

- [x] **ZOOM-01**: Guest can pinch-to-zoom on lightbox images on mobile (two-finger spread to zoom in, pinch to zoom out) — ✓ Delivered in Phase 10 via yarl Zoom plugin with `pinchZoomV4: true`
- [x] **ZOOM-02**: Guest can use scroll-wheel to zoom in and out on lightbox images on desktop — ✓ Delivered in Phase 10 via `scrollToZoom: true`
- [x] **ZOOM-03**: Guest can drag to pan a zoomed-in lightbox image on any device — ✓ Delivered in Phase 10 via yarl Zoom plugin (drag-to-pan built-in)
